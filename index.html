A little help for your shelf.

Support for Cat's Shelf, your personal book collection and reading tracker for iPhone.

Get in touch

Email ccatherinetran@gmail.com for questions, feedback, or help with the app.

For a technical issue, include your iPhone model, iOS version, and what happened. Please leave out private library information unless it is needed to explain the issue.

Adding and organizing books

Tap the plus button to search for a title, author, or ISBN. Choose a book, set its reading status, and save. Use tags to organize books into Collections and personal ratings to browse Ranking.

Importing from Goodreads

Export your library from the Goodreads import/export page and save the CSV file. In Cat's Shelf, open Settings → Import Books → Import Goodreads CSV, choose that file, review the preview, and tap Import.

New books retain their reading status, ratings, finished dates, and shelves as tags. Existing books are skipped without replacing their saved details. Goodreads ratings come from your saved data or export; the App Store version does not retrieve live Goodreads ratings or search results.

Reading progress and Tracker

For books marked Reading or Dropped, set the percentage you have read. Books with a finished date appear automatically in the corresponding month of the yearly Tracker.

Your saved library

Your library and settings are stored on your device. Cat's Shelf does not provide an account or a developer-hosted library backup. App data may be included in your device backups, depending on your Apple settings.

Contact support before deleting or reinstalling the app if you need help protecting your saved library. Books removed within the app can be restored from Recently Deleted for up to 30 days.

Search and covers

Book search, catalog information, and hosted covers are provided by Open Library. An internet connection is needed for these features. Availability varies; books without a matching cover use a fallback tile. You can choose a custom cover from your device.

Book details link back to Open Library, and Settings includes source credits and icon licenses. Saved library records are retained when the app updates, even when a previously used remote cover source is no longer displayed.
