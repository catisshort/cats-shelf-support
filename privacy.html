Privacy policy

Last updated November 9, 2026

Cat's Shelf is a personal book organization and reading tracker app developed by Catherine Tran.

Information stored on your device

Your saved books, reading statuses, progress, personal ratings, completion dates, tags, selected custom covers, and app preferences are stored locally on your device. The app does not provide a Cat's Shelf account or a developer-operated server for storing your library.

Device backups may include app data according to your Apple device and backup settings.

Book searches and online content

The app connects directly to Open Library to search for books, retrieve catalog information, and display hosted cover images. It does not automatically query Google Books, Apple Books, or Goodreads. Saved or imported Goodreads ratings and external Goodreads links remain available.

Online requests may include your search terms, book titles, authors, ISBNs, Open Library identifiers, and ordinary network information such as your IP address and request headers. Open Library handles requests under its own policies. Visible remote covers load when needed; catalog lookups occur when you search or open book details. The app caches catalog responses and does not scan your library for background lookups.

Open Library/Internet Archive may retain request information for service operation, security, diagnostics, and usage statistics. Its published reader privacy policy describes IP-address protections, with exceptions for troubleshooting and attacks. Such logs may contain information associated with a network address. Cat's Shelf does not upload your entire library or connect your local reading records to an online account.

Opening an external link may take you to another app or website, including Goodreads. This policy does not govern those services.

Goodreads file imports

You may choose a Goodreads CSV export from Files. The app reads the selected file on your device and imports book details, reading statuses, ratings, dates, and shelves. The file is not uploaded to a developer-operated server. After import, remote cover images or book information may be requested using the imported book identifiers as described above.

Custom cover images

You can select an image to use as a custom book cover. The selected image is processed and stored locally as part of your library. Cat's Shelf does not upload it to a developer-operated server.

Advertising and tracking

Cat's Shelf does not include advertising or analytics SDKs and does not access the advertising identifier. The developer does not operate a tracking service for this app.

Managing your data

You can edit or remove books and tags in the app. Removed books are kept in Recently Deleted for up to 30 days and can be restored during that period. Deleting the app removes its on-device data; your device backups may retain prior copies under Apple's backup settings.

Contact and support

If you contact support by email, the developer receives your email address and the information you choose to include, and uses that information to respond to your request.

For privacy questions or app support, email ccatherinetran@gmail.com.
